# Jesty RP Charging Separation v1.5.12

### What's new
- **Better updates:** Always-visible **Check Updates** button, with retry and clearer status messages.
- **Battery temperature records:** See the minimum and maximum temperatures measured by the app, and reset them whenever you like.

### Good to know
- Your existing charging settings are kept; bypass charging works as before.
- Temperature records cover only readings taken while the app or its active service is monitoring, not the battery's lifetime.
